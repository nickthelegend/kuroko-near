# Kuroko brand (rebuild, Nov 13 2026)

Kuroko (黒子) are the black-clad stagehands of Japanese theatre: they move the set in full view and the audience agrees not to see them. Kuroko the product is the same idea for money: **your USDC works backstage**. A vault takes deposits, a solver fills other people's swaps on NEAR Intents inside rules the depositors vote on, and the spread is meant to flow back to the share price.

## Type
Gantari (sans: UI and big light numerals with faded trailing decimals) and Fira Mono (hashes and account IDs only).

## Art direction
Editorial still-life photography on a dark stage: matte black objects (a vault, coins, a swap token, a ballot, a relay
antenna, a curtain) lit by one warm orange spotlight from above, deep soft shadows, a faint warm haze in the light cone.
Calm, expensive and serious, like a luxury hardware photo. No face, no people or hands, no kabuki masks, no glow orbs,
no neon, no toy look, no text, letters, numbers or logos.
