# Kuroko art direction
- Style: dramatic kabuki-theatre stagecraft rendered as moody 3D: black-clad stagehand silhouette (kuroko) moving glowing blue order cards on a dark stage, single electric-blue spotlight, smoke, graphite surfaces.
- Palette: #0e0e10 graphite, #18181b, #4fb3f2 electric blue, #9ad3fa soft blue, faint #5cd6a0 for fills.
- Subjects: a hooded stagehand silhouette from behind holding a glowing quote card, a stage with floating price cards, a theatre curtain parting to show order books (abstract bars), a spotlight on an empty stage (empty state).
- Banned: real logos, readable text, faces.
