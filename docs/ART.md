# Kuroko art direction
- Style: dramatic kabuki-theatre stagecraft rendered as moody 3D: a black-clad stagehand silhouette (kuroko) moving glowing persimmon quote cards on a dark stage, one warm persimmon spotlight, smoke, sumi-black surfaces.
- Palette (the kabuki joshiki-maku curtain): sumi #1b1310, persimmon #f06a2a, moegi green #88a83a, persimmon-tinted paper #fcf0e8. No blue, no purple, no teal.
- Subjects: a hooded stagehand from behind holding a glowing quote card (hero), a stage of floating price cards, a curtain parting on abstract bars (rules), a spotlight on an empty stage (no fills yet).
- Banned: real logos, readable text, faces.
- The files in `packages/web/public/art/` were generated in electric blue on 2026-11-09 and recoloured to persimmon on 2026-11-10 with a hue remap of the blue band (no new generation). Regenerate natively in persimmon when convenient.
